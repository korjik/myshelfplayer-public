My Shelf Player User Privacy Choices

Last updated: May 3, 2026

My Shelf Player is designed for self-hosted audiobook libraries. The app does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. This page summarizes the privacy choices available to users.

App Data

  • Delete downloaded audiobooks in the app to remove offline audio files from your device.
  • Delete bookmarks in the app if you no longer want them stored locally.
  • Delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data.

Apple Watch Sync

In the iPhone app settings, you can control whether History and Bookmarks sync to Apple Watch and how many books are included in the watch sync payload.

Audiobookshelf Server Data

Your Audiobookshelf server is controlled by you or your server administrator. To delete server-side account data, playback progress, or library records, use your Audiobookshelf server settings or contact your server administrator.

Apple Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. You can manage purchase history, subscriptions, family sharing, refunds, and Apple Account settings through Apple’s account and App Store settings.

Tracking

My Shelf Player does not ask to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can manage Apple’s app tracking controls in iOS Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the App Store support link for My Shelf Player.
