My Shelf Player Support

For help with My Shelf Player, email myshelfplayer@kozhokaru.com.

Helpful Details To Include

  • Your device model and iOS or watchOS version.
  • The My Shelf Player app version.
  • Whether the issue affects iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, CarPlay, or downloads.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether your Audiobookshelf server is reachable from the device.

Privacy

Do not include your password or server authentication token in support emails. For privacy details, read the Privacy Policy.
